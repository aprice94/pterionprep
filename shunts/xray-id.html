First-look sorting

Round titanium disc with a rotor or dial
Miethke adjustable valves — proGAV / proGAV 2.0 (four rotor magnets in pairs), M.blue (rim of coding holes with a triangular pointer), proSA.
Two square magnets in a faint round body + arrow connectors
Sophysa — Polaris (5 positions, magnetic lock) or Sophy SM8 (8 positions). Radiopaque dots left of the inlet give the pressure range.
Small ring (cam) with a dot
Codman HAKIM programmable — read the marker against the right-hand-side marker; 70 / 120 / 170 sit on the central cross.
Short bright bars around a small rotor
Codman CERTAS Plus — the setting is the sector holding the magnet with the tantalum ball.
A single dense magnet, valve body barely visible
Medtronic Strata family (Strata II / NSC); StrataMR II adds an extra tantalum marker.
Almost nothing — only arrows or dots
Non-metallic Medtronic Delta or PS Medical CSF-Flow Control valve: read the radiopaque arrow / dot code.

Film technique

  • Shoot perpendicular to the valve, with the non-implanted side of the head on the plate; position the film relative to the valve, not the patient's anatomy (Integra Hakim guide).
  • Check orientation markers before reading a dial — a mirror-image read gives the wrong setting (Hakim right-hand-side marker; Miethke valve-marking recess; Sophysa range dots left of the inlet).
  • Get a baseline film after implantation. After every MRI, confirm the setting of MRI-sensitive valves — Codman HAKIM (X-ray or programmer), Strata / Strata NSC (Medtronic tools or X-ray) and Sophy SM8 (adjustment kit or X-ray).
  • Compare with the patient's own baseline films — an unknown outside valve should be identified before anyone tries to adjust it.
