Quick unit converter

1 mmHg = 13.595 mmH₂O = 1.3595 cmH₂O · 1 cmH₂O = 0.7356 mmHg · 1 mmH₂O = 9.807 Pa. Miethke rounds to 1 mmHg ≈ 1.36 cmH₂O; Sophysa to 1 mmH₂O ≈ 0.074 mmHg.

How the converter works

  • Lying comparisons use each valve's nominal opening pressure — the differential-pressure unit alone for gravitational valves.
  • Upright comparisons use: the same pressure for valves with no posture device; the manufacturer's −50 cmH₂O hydrostatic chart for Medtronic Delta-chamber valves (Strata II, StrataMR II, Delta); DP + gravitational unit for Miethke gravitational valves and add-ons; +200 mmH₂O for Sophysa SiphonX; the high-pressure chamber for the DualSwitch valve.
  • Where a manufacturer publishes a range (e.g., Medtronic 5 → 50 mL/h), the midpoint is used as nominal — the same value Medtronic's StrataVarius displays — and the range is shown.
  • Flow-regulating valves (OSV II, M.flow), the flow-activated SiphonGuard, and valves without published per-setting pressures (Integra DP, Spitz-Holter, Integra GCA) are in the valve library but cannot be converted.
  • Ties go to the lower pressure. Every value traces to a manufacturer document or FDA record linked in the valve library.
